# Website PRD — The Athletes Prayer Foundation

## 1. Overview & Objectives
**The Athletes Prayer Foundation (TAP)** is an Atlanta-based 501(c)(3) social enterprise providing a "spiritual anchor" for elite competitors. This website serves as the primary gateway for professional athletes, athletic directors, and families to access TAP’s signature mentorship and the **TAP Time Capsule**—a tangible, career-long spiritual archive.

### Measurable Goals:
*   **Revenue:** Achieve a sustainable $10,000/month run rate within 12 months.
*   **Lead Gen:** Secure 10 institutional high school/collegiate partnership inquiries per quarter.
*   **Conversion:** Maintain a 40% referral-to-subscriber conversion rate for professional athletes.
*   **Reach:** Establish a digital presence that supports active chaplaincy in 8 pro cities (3 NBA, 3 MLB, 2 MLS).

## 3. Brand System
### Visual Tokens
*   **Primary Color (Ebony):** `#1A1A1B` (Backgrounds, primary text, "The Anchor").
*   **Secondary Color (Gold):** `#C5A059` (Logos, CTAs, "The Medal of Faith").
*   **Accent Color (Parchment):** `#F4F1EA` (Card backgrounds, scroll mockups).
*   **Muted Color (Slate):** `#4A4E54` (Secondary text, borders).

### Typography
*   **Headings:** *Playfair Display* (Serif). Evokes prestige, heritage, and the "historical scroll."
*   **Body:** *Montserrat* (Sans-Serif). Modern, clean, and athletic.

### Imagery & Tone
*   **Imagery:** "Chiaroscuro" style—high contrast, dramatic light/shadow. Silhouettes in tunnels, close-ups of calligraphed parchment, wooden locker benches.
*   **Tone:** The "Voice in the Tunnel." Quiet intensity, grounded, prestigious, and professional. 
*   **Avoid:** "Churchy" clip-art, neon/fluorescent sports colors, frantic "hype" language, and religious jargon.
