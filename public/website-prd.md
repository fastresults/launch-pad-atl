# Website PRD: The Athletes Prayer Foundation

This blueprint provides exact parameters to build a high-converting, spiritually-grounded platform for The Athletes Prayer Foundation using an AI website builder. It covers brand aesthetics, page architecture, program pricing tiers, and technical logistics to capture professional athletes, athletic directors, and sponsors.

## AI Website Builder Prompt & Brand Design

Instruct an AI builder to design a responsive, high-performance platform targeting professional and collegiate athletes. The aesthetic is "Elite Performance meets Quiet Reflection"—resembling Nike Lab mixed with a high-end apothecary. Apply the predefined brand tokens: use Cormorant Garamond for elegant, authoritative headings, and Lato for clean, modern body text. Set the global background to deep charcoal (`#231F20`) with primary navigational elements in deep blue (`#04476D`) and sharp accents in red (`#CF3D3E`). The visual layout must prioritize high-definition, softly-lit imagery of parchment scrolls resting on locker room benches beside cleats or professional basketballs. Tone must completely avoid soft, "churchy" jargon. Instead, use high-performance vocabulary like "anchor," "archive," "equipment," and "precision." The primary global call-to-action button should read "Secure Your Anchor" or "Start Your Archive Today."

## Homepage Structure & Core Messaging

Build a three-part homepage narrative centered on identity. The hero section must feature the primary H1: "Your identity is more than your jersey." The subheading should immediately tackle the athlete retirement crisis, noting that 78% of professional athletes face an identity collapse within five years of retirement, and position the foundation as the preventative spiritual anchor. Below the fold, feature "The 78% Crisis" section outlining the mental vacuum created by physical performance alone. Transition directly into a "Signature Solution" block, introducing the tangibility of the TAP Time Capsule and its secure locker room delivery. Conclude the homepage with three distinct audience pathways configured as clickable cards: "The High-Stakes Pro" (NBA, MLB, NFL), "The Rising Student-Athlete" (NCAA, Elite High School), and "The Intentional Parent." Ground the organization's credibility by calling out local Atlanta infrastructure like Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

## Program Tiers & Time Capsule Showcase Page

Create a dedicated product showcase page for the TAP Time Capsule. The page must emphasize the tactile reality of the heavy parchment prayer scroll over digital apps or ephemeral group huddles. Frame it as "The Spiritual Equipment Every Athlete Needs" to build a career-long physical archive. Underneath the product breakdown, integrate three clear membership and pricing cards. Card one: Pro Individual ($85-120/game), featuring direct-to-locker delivery and one-on-one credentialed chaplaincy. Card two: Student-Athlete ($45-85/game), focusing on identity building and resilience. Card three: Legacy Gift Edition ($150-250), functioning as a direct e-commerce purchase for parents or families. Use dedicated layout modules to reassure high-profile athletic directors and equipment managers that delivery logistics integrate perfectly with stadium operations without disrupting game-day workflows.

## Technical Integrations, Lead Routing & SEO

Equip the backend to easily manage diverse user intents and 501(c)(3) operational requirements. The primary lead capture forms must include dropdown routing logic to automatically segment inquiries from an "Athlete," "Agent," "Parent," or "Athletic Director." Integrate Stripe or Shopify to securely handle e-commerce checkout flows for the $150 to $250 Gift Edition sales. For tax-deductible donor contributions, embed Givebutter or Donorbox widgets accessible via a global "Donate" button in the utility navigation. Set the global meta title to "The Athletes Prayer | Spiritual Support for Pro & College Athletes." Inject target localized SEO keywords, including "athlete spiritual wellness Atlanta" and "sports chaplaincy Georgia," into heading tags and image alt texts. The DOM must be heavily mobile-optimized to serve agents and athletes who browse almost exclusively on iOS devices while traveling.
