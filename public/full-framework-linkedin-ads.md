# LinkedIn advertising copy — the full framework

Ad companion to `full-framework-animation-brief.md`. Everything here is paste-ready for Campaign Manager.

Destination for every unit: **startuplabs.online**

House rules held throughout: say *startup*, not "business". Say *asset*, not "document". Say *framework*, not "template". Never call the offer a plan, blueprint, playbook, roadmap, or deliverables — it's a done-with-you build, so name the real artifact (entity filed, page published, first message sent). Price appears at most once per unit.

---

## 1. Targeting and setup

**Geography.** Atlanta metro, plus a 30-mile radius around Norcross, GA. Exclude nothing inside the perimeter — the IGNITE Center pulls from both sides.

**Audiences to build:**

| Audience | Build it from |
|---|---|
| First-time founders | Interests: entrepreneurship, small business ownership, side projects. Job seniority: entry–manager. Exclude Founder/Owner titles. |
| Plan-B seekers | Titles in ops, sales, nursing, teaching, logistics, skilled trades. Tenure 3+ years in current company. Member traits: career-change interest. |
| Main Street and trades operators | Company size 1–10. Industries: construction, consumer services, food & beverage, retail, personal care. Titles: Owner, Operator, Proprietor. |
| Family and couple operators | Company size 1–10, Owner titles, plus interests in franchising and small business finance. |
| Retargeting — video | 50%+ viewers of the 60s film, last 90 days. |
| Retargeting — site | startuplabs.online visitors, last 30 days, excluding anyone who reached the confirmation page. |

**Objectives and placement.**
- Run the 60-second 16:9 film on a **Video views** objective to build the retargeting pool. Feed only.
- Run **Website conversions** on Single Image and the 15s 9:16 cutdown, optimized for the seat form completion.
- The 6-second bumper runs only against warm audiences — it has no room to explain anything cold.
- Turn LinkedIn Audience Network **off** for the conversion campaigns. Leave it on for video views.

**Copy geometry.** Intro text folds at roughly 210 characters on desktop and 150 on mobile — the hook has to land before that. Headline is capped at 70 characters. Two to three hashtags inline, never more.

## 4. Thought-leader posts (organic, then boost)

### Post 1 — The napkin

> A guy showed me a napkin last month. Folded four times, soft at the creases, two years old.
>
> The idea on it was good. It had been good for two years. What he was missing wasn't insight — it was a morning where somebody sat next to him and made him decide things. Who it's for. What it costs. What happens after someone says yes.
>
> That's most of the gap. Not talent, not capital, not a better idea. Just the absence of a room where the decisions get made and written down while you're still sitting there.
>
> So we made the room. Eight phases in one morning at the IGNITE Center in Norcross. You bring the idea and a laptop. You leave with the entity filed, the page published, and the first message sent — and a Second Brain holding every decision, so future-you can ask why.
>
> He filed the LLC before lunch. The napkin's in a drawer now.
>
> #Entrepreneurship #Founders #Atlanta

---

### Post 2 — Against the binder

> I've sat through the workshops that end with a binder. Everyone leaves inspired. Almost nobody ships.
>
> The problem isn't motivation. It's that a binder moves the hardest part — actually building the thing — to a Tuesday night when you're tired and alone and every decision is suddenly yours again.
>
> So we inverted it. Nothing gets explained that doesn't also get built in the room. Positioning gets written, not discussed. The offer gets priced, not "considered." The site goes live on your domain with a working form, not sketched. The first fifty names get listed with actual names on them.
>
> The measure I care about is embarrassingly simple: can you take money on the way home? If not, we didn't do our job.
>
> One morning, Norcross, small on purpose.
>
> #SmallBusiness #Founders #Entrepreneurship
