# Social Copy Blocs — Coffee Cup Ad (1:1)

Match the ad's mood: warm, unhurried, "pull up a chair" — never hype, never countdown-clock urgency. Every bloc ends with a soft, human invitation instead of a hard CTA.

**Deliverable:** `public/social-ad-1x1-copy.md` — five ready-to-paste blocs (caption + gentle CTA + hashtag set), each tuned for a different platform/voice.

---

## Voice guardrails
- Lowercase-friendly, conversational rhythm.
- No "LIMITED SEATS!!" energy. Scarcity is real but stays quiet.
- Lead with the invitation, not the price. Price appears once, near the end.
- Every CTA sounds like a friend holding the door open: *"come sit with us," "save your chair," "pull up a seat," "meet us for coffee."*
- Name-drop the IGNITE Center at Greater Atlanta Christian School once per bloc where it fits naturally.
- Anchor link: `startuplabs.online`

---

## The five blocs

**Bloc 1 — Instagram / Facebook feed (main)**
Warm hero caption, 3–4 short lines, one emoji max. Ends with "save your seat over coffee."

**Bloc 2 — LinkedIn**
Slightly more grown-up. Speaks to the W-2 escapee / side-hustler. No emojis. Ends with "grab a chair — coffee's on us."

**Bloc 3 — Threads / X**
Punchy, one-breath. Two short lines. Ends with "come build it with us."

**Bloc 4 — Instagram Story / short-form (overlay-ready)**
Six lines max, each line stands alone so it works as sticker text over the image. Ends with "tap to pull up a chair."

**Bloc 5 — Community / neighborhood post (Nextdoor, local FB groups, church bulletin tone)**
Plain-spoken, no jargon, mentions Atlanta + IGNITE Center by name. Ends with "come have coffee with us and leave with a startup."

Each bloc includes:
- Caption
- Soft CTA line
- 5–8 tag/hashtag suggestions tuned to that platform
