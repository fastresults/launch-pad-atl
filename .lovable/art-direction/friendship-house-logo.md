# The Friendship House — Logo Art Direction (Tricolore)

Venture: The Friendship House, Château-Thierry, France — a Franco-American integration institution: predictive guidance through French administration, plus a physical residency house.

## The palette this mark is drawn in

| Role | Hex | Use in the mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bleu | `#0055A4` | The mark's structural ink — the form itself |
| Navy | `#002654` | Knockout background; institutional applications |
| Rouge | `#EF4135` | One element only — never a second red |
| Blanc | `#FFFFFF` | Counterform. The white stripe is *cut*, never printed |

Two-ink rule: bleu plus rouge on white. The tricolour supplies the ink; it never supplies the idea.

## Idea: the threshold

The mark is a doorway — a house form whose opening is the subject, not the roof. A threshold says both things the business does at once: an administration crossed, and a house you can actually walk into. The white of the flag becomes the opening, so the brand's welcome is literally negative space.

Second read: the opening should also scan as a upright rectangle of light — an arrival, a page, a permit granted.

## Construction rules

- One stroke weight throughout. Geometric, not hand-drawn; the institution must look reliable.
- Optical squaring: the mark sits in a near-square field, never a wide badge.
- Rouge lands on exactly one element — a lintel, a threshold bar, or the door's edge. Never two.
- Counterform does the work: no more than three closed shapes total.
- No perspective, no gradients, no bevels, no outline-plus-fill duplication.

## Forbidden

- Drawing the French flag, a flag in a house, a flag on a pole, or three equal vertical stripes.
- The Eiffel Tower, the Arc de Triomphe, fleur-de-lys, coq gaulois, Marianne.
- Two flags, two hands shaking, two halves of a heart, globes with pins.
- Any US flag element. The brand is French-hosted; the American side is the audience, not the identity.

## Reductions (all four must be drawn)

1. Full colour: bleu with one rouge element on white.
2. One-ink bleu `#0055A4`.
3. One-ink black.
4. Knockout white on navy `#002654`.

At 24 px the mark must still read as a door.

## Lockup and typography

- Wordmark: Montserrat 700, tracking tightened to roughly −15, set in title case.
- Always "The Friendship House" in full. No initials, no "TFH", no bilingual second line inside the lockup.
- Horizontal lockup: mark left, wordmark right, optical baseline aligned to the mark's opening, gap equal to the mark's stroke width × 4.
- Stacked lockup: mark centred over the wordmark, gap equal to the cap height.
- Clear space: the width of the door opening on all four sides.
- Minimums: 150 px digital, 1.5 in print.

## Backgrounds

Approved: white, `#F4F6F9`, navy `#002654`. On navy the mark reverses to white with the rouge element retained. The mark is never placed on rouge, on photography without a scrim, or on any surface where the bleu drops below 4.5:1 against its ground.
